How this estimate is built: Federal poverty guidelines and IRS applicable-percentage table are exact (2025 HHS guidelines & Rev. Proc. 2025-25, used for 2026 plan-year coverage). The benchmark premium is a state-level average second-lowest-cost silver premium for a 40-year-old (KFF analysis of CMS QHP Rate/Landscape data), age-adjusted using the federal default 3:1 age curve — not an actual county-level quote. Metal-tier prices use typical national ratios to the benchmark silver plan. Real premiums vary by county, carrier, and plan; cost-sharing reductions, state-based marketplace subsidies (CA/NJ/NY/MA/VT/WA), and non-expansion Medicaid gap rules are not modeled. This tool is for directional planning only — not a quote, and not tax or legal advice.

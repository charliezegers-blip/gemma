Decisions the board will need to make together

  1. Do members still get something at the door? If entry is free for everyone, the membership pitch loses its most concrete benefit. Test this with the membership change in Option C.
  2. Who pays for parking? Member discounts protect renewals but cut parking income. Event guests are often the largest parking group.
  3. Does a parking charge dampen giving? Visitors who pay to park may feel they have already paid. The donation fatigue setting lets you test that.
  4. What does the museum cost to open? Admission pays for animal care. Free entry only works if donations cover that same cost.
